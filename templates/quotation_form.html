Create a Quotation

Prepare a clear, itemised offer for a buyer. Select the sourcing request and supplier, enter your costs, and review the proposed selling price before saving the draft.

Important: approval comes first

Saving a draft does not mean the buyer has approved the order. Confirm the quotation terms and wait for the buyer's approval before purchasing the product.
